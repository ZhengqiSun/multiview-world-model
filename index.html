02The shared world

A world is not a collection of videos. It is a single state that remains true from wherever you stand.

Traditional video models imagine one camera at a time. Our model reasons about a shared environment, then generates each observation as a view into that same evolving reality.

01

Shared state

Every viewpoint is conditioned on one coherent account of the world.

02

Situated views

Each observer sees a distinct perspective without breaking the shared scene.

03

Persistent time

The world carries its history forward instead of restarting at every clip.
